# Staff guide

Use only the authorized live app for real students. A gold **FICTIONAL DEMO** banner means practice data only.

**Try the proof of concept:** open **https://art-class-checkin-poc.vercel.app** and choose **Enter demo as staff**. Select **After-School Art Studio** and a dated session, or create a new session. Use **Enter demo as app administrator** when practicing setup and corrections. These demonstration roles need no Google or Vercel login; enter fictional information only. On the installed demo icon, choose a demo role again if its session expires. The live sign-in instructions below apply only after a later live deployment.

1. **Before class:** connect to the internet, open the app, sign in through Differance Labs if asked, and select the correct dated class under **Today**. Tap **Print backup** and print both the attendance roster and the separate staff pickup/contact reference. Keep both under staff control.
2. **Drop-off:** search the child's name. At the physical handoff to staff, tap **Check in**. Wait for server confirmation and **Present**. A child remains Present until an actual release is recorded, even after midnight.
3. **Paid:** independently confirm payment in Square, Venmo, cash records or another method, then check the clearly labeled **Paid** box. Wait for **Confirmed**. **Details** accepts optional method, amount, actual payment date and note. Unchecked means **Not confirmed**, not a debt. Payment never blocks a handoff. Clearing/correcting a confirmation requires a reason and never issues a refund.
4. **Pickup:** tap **Pick up**, choose a currently approved adult, then **Known to staff** or **Photo ID checked**. Inspect the optional reference portrait if needed. Never photograph or store an ID. At the physical handoff, tap **Confirm release** and wait for **Released**. Repeat separately for each sibling. Parents need no phone.
5. **Adult not listed:** call the parent's number already stored in the app. An app administrator must verify authorization and change the child's permission with a confirmation note. An emergency contact alone is not approved for pickup. Do not release until current permission is recorded and verified.
6. **Absence:** under the child's **More** menu choose **Mark absent** when appropriate. Paid may still be confirmed for an absent student. If they later arrive, **Check in** records their handoff.
7. **Connection trouble:** do not assume an operation succeeded. Follow **Check unconfirmed saves → Check save status**. Retry only with the app's **Retry same request** control after checking. Use paper if saves cannot be confirmed; the app does not silently queue handoffs.
8. **After paper use:** open the original dated roster, then the child's **More → Enter paper attendance**. Enter actual arrival/departure, the adult and verification written on paper, and staff initials when available. Leave times blank if you are preserving an existing digital event. Check **Paper records a payment confirmation** only when the paper confirms payment; enter its original confirmation time if known. A blank paper Paid box leaves digital payment unchanged. Tap **Reconcile paper record** and inspect History.
9. **Conflicts/mistakes:** compare the saved record with the paper and the other staff member. An app administrator uses **More → Correct attendance**, supplies the corrected state/times and a required reason. For a paper discrepancy, the admin documents the historical authorization/reconciliation note. Original events remain in History. Revoke current adult permission separately when needed.
10. **End of class:** inspect every remaining Present child and resolve their actual whereabouts/handoff before closing. Check History, filtered to this session. Export CSV if needed. Reconcile sheets, store them securely under the owner's retention policy, and sign out on a shared device.

New students, contacts, approved adults, archived records and future class rosters are managed by an **app administrator** under Students and Classes. Updating a class changes future dated sessions only. Each new session starts Not confirmed.

If the installed icon asks you to sign in again, use **Sign in to this Home Screen app**. Keep the displayed code open; in Safari sign in through Differance Labs, launch this app, choose **Account → Connect Home Screen device**, and approve the code from your own device. Return to the icon. Codes expire in five minutes and app sessions after twelve hours.

For iPhone/iPad printing, use **Open PDF → Share → Print**, or **Save PDF → Files → open the PDF → Share → Print**. If Home Screen printing is unavailable, open the stable address in Safari, sign in, and use Print backup there. Downloaded PDFs and CSVs contain private information; never leave them accessible to parents.
